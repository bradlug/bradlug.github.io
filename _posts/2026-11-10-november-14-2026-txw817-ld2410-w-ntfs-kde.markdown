Scheifler announcing the development of X from W.]</p><p><strong>David</strong> mentioned how Windows <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NTFS" type="text/html" role="link">NTFS</a> incorporated aspects of <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OpenVMS||VMS" type="text/html" role="link">VMS</a> after <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dave_Cutler" type="text/html" role="link">Dave Cutler</a> left DEC and went to Microsoft in 1988; DEC sued and got $200,000,000 in Microsoft licences. David added that part of the suspicion was because QT was an incubator under <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canopy_Group" type="text/html" role="link">Canopy Group</a> along with the <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SCO_Group" type="text/html" role="link">SCO Group</a>.</p><p><strong>Mike</strong> mentioned <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Starlink" type="text/html" role="link">Starlink</a> which uses lots of low altitude satellites, mostly at the central latitudes, which use lasers to create a mesh; ground control needs to know its latitude to communicate.</p>
