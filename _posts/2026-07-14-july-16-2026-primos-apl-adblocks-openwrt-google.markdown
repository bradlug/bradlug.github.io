<p><strong>David</strong>, in response to a comment about virtualisation, mentioned that <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PRIMOS" type="text/html" role="link">PRIMOS</a> could be run as a user session under PRIMOS but had no access to services. It was written in <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fortran" type="text/html" role="link">Fortran</a> and was used by accountants in one department in which he worked.</p><p><strong>John</strong> remarked that, once <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/VisiCalc" type="text/html" role="link">VisiCalc</a> and <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lotus_1-2-3" type="text/html" role="link">Lotus 1-2-3</a> arrived, accountants swapped to using those. Darren asked about accounting programs which did not rely on a spreadsheet and John said that there is <a href="https://kmymoney.org/" type="text/html" role="link">KMyMoney</a> for personal accounting and <a href="https://gnucash.org/" type="text/html" role="link">GNUCash</a> for business accounting.</p><p><strong>Darren</strong> has been working on coding <a href="https://conwaylife.com/" type="text/html" role="link">Conway’s Game of Life</a> in APL; he has got this far</p><figure style="justify-items: center;"><img src="https://www.bradlug.co.uk/blog/2026/07/14/images/Game_of_life.png" width="424" height="339" role="img"></figure><p>but rotate in the Neighbours function does not work in the new versions of <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/APL_(programming_language)" type="text/html" role="link">APL</a>.</p><p><strong>John</strong> mentioned that the German Federal Government has been working towards the adoption of open standards and from 2027 will only accept ODF and PDF/UA for documents.</p><p>There was a discussion of the various software which incorporates Adblocking either inherently or if the user selects it. John mentioned <a href="https://apps.kde.org/en-gb/plasmatube/" type="text/html" role="link">PlasmaTube</a> which relies on <a href="https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp" type="text/html" role="link"><code>yt-dlp</code></a> and David noted that <code>yt-dlp</code> will download videos from almost any webpage.</p><p><strong>Brian</strong> mentioned that you can now use <a href="https://www.home-assistant.io/" type="text/html" role="link">Home Assistant</a> to handle intercoms and telephones. However, he recently had a problem after updating <a href="https://openwrt.org/" type="text/html" role="link">OpenWRT</a>; the Wi-Fi coverage was terrible and he had to roll back to 3.8.</p><p>In order to connect to the Internet, he uses a <a href="https://smarty.co.uk/" type="text/html" role="link">Smarty</a> SIM to connect to 5G; the advantage of Smarty is that contracts are monthly and so he can vary the contract each month depending on whether he is going to be in Spain or in England. That said Smarty deals for individuals are quite good and he has opted for one of those deals at present. It was noted that Smarty is part of 3 which had joined Vodafone who often lay down quite tight conditions. However David looked up the current Smarty conditions and found that they were quite generous, more than enough for Brian’s needs.</p><p>Brian also noted that Google is changing the way you can install apps on Android, to the disadvantage of software like <a href="https://f-droid.org/" type="text/html" role="link">F-Droid</a>, while David pointed out that the European Court had recently <a href="https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cvgj0pp5p62o" type="text/html" role="link">upheld a €4.1bn fine</a> for using the Android system to block rivals.</p>
